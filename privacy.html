Privacy Policy

Whisker Tech · last updated 2025-10-25

Whisker Tech is a creator channel that publishes short videos about AI tools on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and Snapchat. To publish its own videos, the channel uses a private tool ("the Publisher") that connects only to the channel owner's own accounts.

Who uses the Publisher

Only the owner of Whisker Tech. The Publisher is not offered to the public, and it never signs in to, reads from or posts to anyone else's account.

What data the Publisher handles

  • Sign-in tokens that the platforms issue after the owner approves access (YouTube, TikTok, Facebook/Instagram). They are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to upload the channel's videos and read basic account information (such as the channel or account name) to confirm the right account is connected.
  • The channel's own videos and their titles, captions and hashtags, which are sent to the platforms when the owner chooses to publish.

The Publisher does not collect, store or process any personal data of viewers or other users. It does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and it does not sell or share data with anyone.

Viewers of our videos

When you watch, like or comment on our videos, that happens on the platform itself (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Snapchat) and is covered by that platform's privacy policy. Whisker Tech only sees the aggregated statistics each platform provides to creators.

Retention and deletion

Sign-in tokens are kept until the owner disconnects an account, and they can be revoked at any time in each platform's connected-apps settings. Published videos stay on the platforms until the owner deletes them.

AI-generated content

Our videos use an AI-generated voice and animated mascot. We label AI-generated content on every platform that supports it.
